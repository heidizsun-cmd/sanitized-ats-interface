Sample hiring process

A clear process, from hello to decision.

No mystery rounds or performative puzzles. We use each conversation to understand how you think, collaborate, and make things useful.

  1. 01Start with context

    A focused conversation about your work, what you want next, and what we are building.

  2. 02Work through something real

    A practical discussion or short exercise shaped around the actual role.

  3. 03Meet the team

    Compare working styles, ask the hard questions, and leave with a clear view of the job.
